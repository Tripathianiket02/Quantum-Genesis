==================================================================

OBJECTIVE

Design and implement a complete observability, diagnostics,
logging and analytics platform in pure MQL5.

This module is responsible for making every important decision,
calculation and execution performed by Project Quantum observable,
traceable, explainable and measurable.

The platform MUST NOT execute trades.

The platform MUST NOT calculate market analysis.

Its sole responsibility is presenting, recording and analyzing
information generated by all other engines.

==================================================================
